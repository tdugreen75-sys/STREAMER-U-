# Streamer U 2026 Campus Plan

## 1. Main Campus
- Main entrance and Streamer U signage
- Large central quad and gathering areas
- Academic buildings
- Campus roads and walkways
- Parking areas
- Landscaping and outdoor spaces
- Assembly/gathering building or space

## 2. Academics
- Classrooms and lecture halls
- Library
- Study areas
- Creator education facilities

### Classes
- Content Creation 101
- Camera Setup & Lighting
- Audio & Streaming Equipment
- Overlay & UI Optimization
- Community Management
- Chat Moderation
- IRL Streaming
- Gaming Content
- Just Chatting Strategies

There is no in-game class schedule system.

## 3. Housing
### Student Dorms
- Two students per room
- Two separate beds on opposite sides of the room
- Students use designated dorm locations rather than random runtime assignment
- Common areas and residential support spaces

### Faculty/Staff Housing
- Dedicated building for professors and club directors
- Offices and dorm/living spaces

## 4. Creator Facilities
- Streaming studios
- Recording rooms
- Podcast rooms
- Green-screen rooms
- Editing labs
- Collaboration spaces

## 5. Student Life & Clubs
- Fraternity
- Basketball
- Cheerleading
- Film
- Science & Engineering
- Musical Arts
- Debate
- Drama

## 6. Administration & Public Safety
- Streamer U Police Department (SUPD)
- SUPD police vehicle facilities
- Faculty/professor and club director building
- Administrative spaces
- Janitor/service spaces
- Student ID and Staff ID support
- Uniform closet system

## 7. Explicitly Excluded
- Creator Hall
- Creator Mansion
- Esports
- NPC professors
- Reputation systems
- Dispatch office
- Interview room
- Evidence area
- Laundry room
- In-game class schedule
